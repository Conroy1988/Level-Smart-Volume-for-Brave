LEVEL PRIVACY POLICY · EFFECTIVE 9 AUGUST 2026

Your audio stays yours.

Level is designed so that its core job does not require us to receive your audio or browsing data.

What Level processes

Only after you activate Level for a tab, Brave provides the extension with that tab's audio stream. Level processes the stream locally in the browser's memory and immediately plays the adjusted audio through your device. Level does not record, retain, transmit, sell, or share the audio.

Information stored on your device

Level stores your settings, listening-mode choices, output percentage, and optional per-website preferences using the browser extension storage API. Active-session state is stored only for the current browser session. You can clear website memory from Level's settings page.

Network activity

Level has no server component and makes no analytics, advertising, account, telemetry, or tracking requests. Its packaged runtime contains no remotely hosted code.

Permissions

  • Tab capture provides the audio for the tab you explicitly activate.
  • Offscreen document keeps local audio processing alive after the popup closes.
  • Tabs and active tab identify the activated tab, show audible tabs, and remember optional domain preferences.
  • Storage saves settings locally in your browser profile.

Third parties and children

Level does not disclose personal data to third parties and is not designed to collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If Level's data practices ever materially change, this policy and the extension's store disclosure will be updated before the changed version is distributed.

Contact

Privacy questions and security reports can be raised through the project's GitHub repository. Security-sensitive reports should use GitHub private vulnerability reporting rather than a public issue.
